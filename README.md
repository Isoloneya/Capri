# Capri Desktop

**Capri is a character who lives on your desktop.**

<p align="center"><img src="assets/capri/capri-cutout.png" width="360" alt="Capri"></p>

She moves between windows, reacts to your actions, falls asleep and retreats into a compact biochip. Expressive animation and vivid effects make your desktop part of her world.

## More than animation

- **A lively character:** movement, dragging, sleep and interactions with window edges.
- **A coding companion:** reactions to Claude Code activity, completed tasks and permission requests when connected.
- **Personal chat:** streaming conversations through Ollama with your selected model.
- **Folders at hand:** “open downloads”, “show documents”, “show desktop”. “What is here?” lists the last selected folder in Capri’s panel; file names stay local.
- **App commands:** ask Capri to open an app immediately; closing still requires a button. Resume or pause Spotify with music commands.
- **Spotify Liked Songs:** ask “play fourth track from liked songs” — Capri opens Spotify and waits for the list. You can also ask “show liked songs”. After a selection, use “next”, “previous”, “now third”, “pause it” or “resume”. Adjacent tracks follow the last confirmed list selection; changing the subject clears shorthand context. Capri reads accessible rows in Spotify and checks playback before reporting success. This is limited Spotify integration, not full desktop vision.
- **Your pace:** adjust size, sound, motion and display, with a reduced-motion option.
- **Two languages:** English and Russian interface, scripted dialogue and demos.

The character works on her own; Claude Code and Ollama are optional integrations.
